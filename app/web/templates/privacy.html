How long we keep it

  • Images and results: until you delete them, or until you delete your account. Deleting an image removes the files from storage.
  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Sessions: expire automatically; deleting your account or resetting your password revokes them all at once.
  • Payment records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account. This is the one thing deletion cannot remove.

Deleting your account

You can do it yourself: Account in the sidebar, then Delete my account, confirmed with your password. What happens:

  • Any active subscription is cancelled immediately, before anything else — a closed account is never left paying.
  • Your images, results and thumbnails are deleted from storage, along with your jobs, sessions and any password-reset links.
  • Your email address and password are erased and your balance is zeroed.
  • The payment records that tax law obliges us to keep survive, no longer linked to a usable account. Your email address is freed, so you can sign up again later if you want.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, have it deleted, take it elsewhere, or object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to {{ fill(legal.contact_email, "LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL") }} and we'll answer within 30 days. If you think we've handled your data badly you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2, session and reset tokens are stored only as hashes, all traffic runs over HTTPS, and every image and download is checked against the account that owns it. No system is perfect: if a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Children

SuperScaler is not for people under 16. We don't knowingly keep accounts for them — tell us if one exists and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, account holders get an email before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
