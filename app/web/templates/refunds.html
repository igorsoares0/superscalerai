The {{ window_days }}-day refund

If the service isn't what you expected, ask within {{ window_days }} days of a charge and we'll refund it in full — no explanation needed, provided the plan's credits are largely unused. Each credit spent is real GPU time that we have already paid for, so if most of the allowance is gone we may refund the unused part instead of everything. We'd rather be generous than argue about it.

If you're in the EU or the UK

You have a statutory right to withdraw within 14 days of purchase. Because the service starts immediately, you accept when you subscribe that processing begins at once; if you then withdraw, we may deduct the value of what you've already used. This right sits alongside the policy above — whichever is better for you applies.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew monthly until you cancel, and we email a receipt every time. You can cancel from the billing panel at any moment:

  • Cancelling stops future charges. You keep your plan and your credits until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Any credits left at that point end with the subscription — they don't carry over.
  • Changed your mind? While a cancellation is still scheduled, the same button resumes the subscription and nothing is charged.

A renewal you forgot about is worth an email: if you cancel right after one and haven't used the new period's credits, we'll refund it.

Failed enhancements

If a job fails, its credits go straight back to your balance — automatically, no request needed. That's a credit refund, not a money refund: the credits are yours to spend on another image.

A result you simply don't like is not a failure. Generative upscaling is not deterministic, and some photos come out better than others — that's why new accounts get free credits to try before paying.

What isn't refunded

  • Billing periods that already ended, beyond the {{ window_days }}-day window.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the terms.

Before you file a chargeback

Please write to us first. A chargeback costs us a fee on top of the refund and freezes the account while the bank decides, so it's slower for you than asking. We answer refund requests within 5 business days; Paddle then takes 5–10 days to put the money back on your card.
